## Final illustration prompt

```text
Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: supporting editorial illustration layer for a Korean Tistory
DuckDB explainer, portrait 4:5.
Input images: Image 1 is a layout-and-explanatory-rhythm reference only; borrow
its clear narrative progression, expressive human scenes, friendly educational
polish, and meaningful color coding, but do not copy its characters, exact
panels, text, or layout. Image 2 is a palette-and-subject reference only;
borrow the warm ivory paper atmosphere, mustard-yellow duck motif, tactile
restraint, and editorial finish, but do not copy the exact composition.
Primary request: Create a polished, original three-stage editorial illustration
that visually explains how DuckDB helps a developer turn an overwhelming local
data file into a small useful result. No embedded text will be used; exact
Korean labels will be added later outside the image.
Creative intent: The viewer should immediately feel the transition from
overwhelmed by a huge file, to focused processing, to calm understanding.
Visual idea: One continuous vertical story with three distinct but connected
scenes. Stage one near the upper area: an adult developer at a desk looks
concerned as a long, dense paper table with many rows and columns unrolls from a
laptop and crowds the workspace. Stage two in the middle and largest area: a
small refined mustard-yellow duck guide moves along the paper table and cleanly
selects only a few colored columns and filtered rows, feeding them through a
precise tabletop sorting path; use visible physical transformation and
directional motion, not floating tech UI. Stage three near the lower area: the
same developer looks relieved while holding a compact, neatly organized result
card, with the unused long paper receding behind. The three scenes must read in
order without labels.
Art direction: contemporary Korean magazine educational illustration,
expressive hand-drawn characters, refined ink contours, soft gouache and
colored-pencil texture, sophisticated rather than childish, commissioned
editorial finish. Friendly explanatory warmth comparable to a professionally
illustrated guidebook.
Composition/framing: portrait 4:5, generous outer margin, clear top-to-bottom
reading path, stage two receives the most visual weight, natural asymmetry,
varied scale, curved directional connectors integrated into the scene, several
clean blank cream callout zones for later typography, no rigid dashboard boxes.
Color palette: warm ivory base, deep navy for structure, mustard yellow for the
duck and selected data, muted teal and coral as small stage accents; calm,
balanced, print-like colors.
Lighting/mood: luminous paper-like daylight, reassuring and intelligent, subtle
shadows and material texture.
Subject details: one consistent adult developer character across stages with
natural hands and expressive but restrained facial expressions; one small
stylized duck guide, not the official DuckDB logo, not a rubber bath toy, not a
mascot sticker. The data must visibly resemble a tabular file through rows and
columns, but contain no readable writing.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no
pseudo-writing, no fake software interface, no charts claiming performance, no
database-cylinder icon, no neon, no 3D plastic, no photorealism. Keep blank
callout areas truly empty and clean for deterministic Korean typography later.
Avoid: generic Canva cards, corporate slide deck, identical repeated boxes,
feature-icon inventory, childish clip art, excessive decoration, crowded tiny
details, rigid symmetry, thick glossy gradients.
```
