- AA Terminal 67% vs manufacturer 65.7%: two publisher results, kept separate. Exact cause not asserted without matched run configuration. No averaged/composite score.

- Korean writing, local account quotas, actual download/file export outcomes, regional billing, 3D printing physical quality remain untested and are not advertised as measured.
- No independent local result on the two models. The article says published/official/independent evaluation as appropriate and labels its constructed prompt and arithmetic example.

## Reproducible arithmetic

Input rate * 100000/1000000 + output rate * 10000/1000000. Kimi: .2+.1=.3. Opus: .4+.2=.6. Cache read .20/MTok equal for three rows. Thinking/reasoning usage belongs in billed output, so same visible answer length does not imply same billed count. Raw machine-readable values are in cost-calculation.json.
