# Higgsfield beginner rewrite — verified research packet

Checked 2026-10-29 UTC. Public official documentation only; no generation or account modification. This is editorial research, not hands-on output evidence.

## Existing article diagnosis

Read the actual published article: https://dop3n.tistory.com/entry/힉스필드-AI란-광고·숏폼-제작-기능과-주목받는-이유

It begins with a good product-photo-to-Reel premise, but then branches into a product catalogue rather than following that task. Main image is an API login page, not the consumer creation interface. Eight sections cover Cinema, Marketing, Soul, MCP/CLI, API, Supercomputer/Apps, funding and users before a practical decision. A beginner still cannot picture what to upload, click, receive, or budget. Platform-versus-model explanation is useful but overtaxonomized. Company growth and API account taxonomy should be cut from the main article. The existing 15-second Marketing limit is not present in the current help page; do not carry it forward unverified.

## Recommended narrative

1. What it is: one website for generating images and short video clips with several AI models; not a single video model competing directly with every model it hosts.
2. One relatable walkthrough: a cafe's own cup/product photo becomes a short moving promotional clip. Distinguish the input photo, chosen example template, and generated output. Do NOT label a template preview as our generated result.
3. At most three entry points: Video for image→clip; Marketing Studio for product templates; Cinema Studio for intentional camera/scene control. Do not enumerate every model/version.
4. Price logic: subscription credits are a usage budget; retrying costs too; Unlimited is a named-model, dated entitlement, not every video for free.
5. Verdict: useful for short visual material and variations; a multi-minute story still needs selecting and arranging clips, consistency review, sound and editing. Frame as workflow inference, not experimentally measured performance.

## Primary source packet

### First-generation instructions
https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/getting-started/how-do-i-create-my-first-generation
Official date Jul 30, 2026. Choose Image or Video, model/feature (default is allowed), enter prompt, upload supported reference, set duration/aspect/resolution, Generate. Result appears in the creation section and Assets. Useful for the simplest general example. Available settings depend on model, so do not prescribe 5/10 seconds or 4K universally.

### Marketing Studio — clean concrete 3-step example
https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/tools/how-do-i-use-marketing-studio-to-create-video-ads
Official date Aug 1, 2026. Marketing Studio → choose template → upload product photo → Recreate. Credit cost is shown before confirmation. Motion, UGC and TikTok categories produce videos; Product Shot, Ads and Marketplace produce images. The template route needs no written prompt. Product Link is another route; if URL extraction fails, upload images directly. UGC voice and pacing can vary. Image results are editable in My generations; changing a video result here requires regeneration. Do not promise every template makes video or is Unlimited. Summarize this source within ~200 English-equivalent words in published copy.

### Cinema Studio — useful secondary path, not giant feature list
https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/tools/how-do-i-use-cinema-studio
Official date Aug 1, 2026. Current default 4.0 runs on Seedance 2.5; up to 50 references and up to 30-second clips, camera/lighting controls, reusable Elements. Supports generating, extending and regional video editing. Those maxima are specific to this tool/version, not all Higgsfield outputs. Reuse is an aid to consistency, not a quality guarantee. Native audio exists, but don't claim voices will stay fixed across generations. Most article readers need only: for choosing camera movement, lighting and scene composition more deliberately.

### Which tool / model defaults
https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/tools/which-higgsfield-tool-should-i-use
Official date Aug 1, 2026. Marketing = product ads from image/URL; Cinema = camera-controlled scenes; most tools can begin on a default model. Site offers multiple model families. Examples in its video navigation include Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1 and Sora 2. No need to compare all models in this introduction.

### Credits
https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/how-credits-work
Official date Aug 3, 2026. Cost depends on model, resolution and duration and is shown on Generate. Generations, re-rolls and upscaling spend credits unless covered by eligible website-only free/Unlimited entitlements. Subscription credits do not roll over; monthly reset on paid renewal, annual every 30 days. Credit Pack/Auto-Refill credits expire 90 days after purchase. General browsing does not use credits. No need to include all expiration taxonomy in beginner article: strongest information is cost-per-attempt + retries + no rollover. Use a clearly labelled fictional calculation if useful, e.g. not a claim that a real clip costs a fixed number.

### Unlimited — crucial nuance
https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/what-are-unlimited-models-and-which-plans-include-them
Official date Aug 3, 2026. Applies to specific models for a stated period, not the entire platform. Both video and image model bundles exist. Eligible generation panel has Unlimited toggle; off means credit mode. Active subscription required. Standard queue for Unlimited, priority for credit mode; speed/concurrency can vary. Fair use is personal human usage: automation/scripts/credential sharing/resale prohibited. Website-only: MCP/CLI/Canvas/Supercomputer generations deduct credits. Thus never say 'agent can create endlessly through the website for you'. Article can compactly say video may be eligible too, but inspect target model + period + toggle; not simply images-only or everything-unlimited.

### Pricing caveat
https://higgsfield.ai/pricing
Public text extraction returned navigation/login only, no current plan amounts or included model lists. No exact dollar or plan-credit claims verified by this research. Screenshot worker may verify actual UI. Prefer not to anchor article on changeable sale/annual-equivalent numbers. Do not copy remembered Pro price from older conversation.

### Commercial use and uploads
https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/account/who-owns-my-generations-and-can-i-use-them-commercially
https://higgsfield.ai/terms-of-use-agreement
Help updated Aug 2, 2026; current Terms inspected sections 4.2–4.4 and 5. Section 4.4 does not restrict commercial use of outputs. Separate rights and consent for uploaded person/brand/copyright content remain user's responsibility (4.2, 5.1, 5.3). Do not turn this into 'all results copyright safe'. General content may be used to improve models; Enterprise agreements differ. Avoid business secrets/uploads in example; a self-owned simple product photo is cleanest. Short paragraph enough, not a legal essay.
