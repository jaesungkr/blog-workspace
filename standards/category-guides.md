# Category guides

## Current taxonomy and routing

Use this category tree unless the user explicitly changes it:

- `Log`
  - `AI 개념 · 실전`
  - `AI 모델 · 비교`
  - `개발 · 디지털`
- `Trends`
  - `AI 뉴스 · 테크 · 산업`
  - `경제 · 금융`
  - `사회 · 문화`
- `Health`
  - `질환 · 의약품`
  - `영양 · 식단`
- `Reflections`
  - `말씀 묵상`
  - `신앙 질문`
  - `성경 읽기`

Classify by the promise made to the reader, not by a noun in the title:

- Route evergreen explanations, tests, comparisons, tutorials, and problem
  solving to `Log`.
- Route event-driven news, company or market changes, history, culture, sports,
  and general-interest trivia to `Trends`.
- Route symptoms, diseases, medicines, supplements, nutrition, and diet guidance
  to `Health`.
- Route sermons, meditation, faith questions, and Bible-reading material to
  `Reflections`.
- When two categories are plausible, use the question that most of the body
  answers. Use tags for the secondary topic.
- Do not create a new subcategory for one isolated post. Prefer the nearest
  current subcategory and split it after roughly 3-5 related posts or when a
  clear publishing pipeline exists.

Examples:

- A model benchmark or hands-on Claude Code guide belongs in `Log`; an AI
  product launch or company restructuring belongs in
  `Trends > AI 뉴스 · 테크 · 산업`.
- Food history, origins, and cultural trivia such as ube belong in
  `Trends > 사회 · 문화`; nutrition effects or dietary recommendations belong
  in `Health > 영양 · 식단`.
- Sports posts currently belong in `Trends > 사회 · 문화`; create a sports
  subcategory only after enough related posts accumulate.

## Log

Use for technology, development, LLMs, tools, experiments, and technical
principles.

Choose the subcategory by intent:

- `AI 개념 · 실전`: AI principles, mechanisms, introductory explanations, and
  hands-on technical experiments.
- `AI 모델 · 비교`: model selection, benchmarks, pricing or capability
  comparisons, and measured evaluations.
- `개발 · 디지털`: programming languages, developer workflows, PC
  troubleshooting, utilities, VPNs, and non-AI digital topics.

- Open conversationally with the reader's problem and signal the tested
  conclusion without using unexplained technical names.
- Before detailed results, explain the compared method families, the practical
  difference between them, the exact test, and what counts as a good outcome.
- For algorithm or model comparisons, trace the calculation from raw input to
  final score. Clarify which methods vectorize, what their vectors represent,
  which comparison metric is used, and which methods follow a different path.
- Prefer direct tests, reproducible steps, official documentation, and
  independent benchmarks.
- Separate vendor-reported results from independent verification.
- Expose setup, conditions, failure cases, cost, and limitations.
- For comparisons, give a compact recommendation by use case and, when the
  evidence allows, name one default starting choice. If showing a practical
  overall rank, state its criteria and keep it separate from rankings observed
  in one test scenario.
- A plain personal observation may close the post when a call to action feels
  forced.

## Trends

Use for AI and technology news, industry, economics, finance, society, culture,
sports, consumer IT, and current events.

Choose the subcategory by intent:

- `AI 뉴스 · 테크 · 산업`: product launches, company moves, regulation
  affecting technology businesses, and timely industry analysis.
- `경제 · 금융`: markets, financial institutions, personal-finance
  implications, and economic policy.
- `사회 · 문화`: history, food culture, social issues, sports, and
  general-interest knowledge that gains value from context.

- Add value beyond the news: history, records, background, trivia, mechanism, or
  a Korean-reader angle.
- Open with the result, then add 1-2 sentences explaining why it matters or
  feels surprising to Korean readers.
- Put heavy political or social analysis in the factual body, not the
  conclusion.
- Close with the Korean connection and 1-2 restrained personal observations.

### Sports within Trends

Use this default structure when it matches the subject:

1. SEO title
2. Result and relevance
3. Brief game summary
4. Key records table
5. Interesting history and context, with meaningful space
6. Restrained personal close

## Health

Use for health, medicine, nutrition, supplements, and diet. Keep Health separate
from Trends even when the topic is timely.

Choose the subcategory by intent:

- `질환 · 의약품`: symptoms, differential warning signs, diseases, medicines,
  safety, interactions, and situation-based drug choices.
- `영양 · 식단`: nutrients, supplements, foods, eating patterns, and
  evidence-based diet guidance.

Use this order when applicable:

1. Practical summary table immediately after the opening
2. Benefits and mechanism
3. Side effects, interactions, and people who should limit or avoid it
4. Forms sold on the market, including compound types
5. Comparison by form and decision criteria

Use current authoritative medical sources. Do not overstate observational
findings or substitute generic disclaimers for precise risk information.

## Reflections

Use for weekly sermon-based meditation. Read `standards/reflections-guide.md`
before planning or writing. Unlike monetized posts, first person, exploratory
flow, a roadmap sentence, and an unresolved question are allowed. Still apply
consistent 존대어, natural flow, and Korean sentence hygiene.

Choose the subcategory by format:

- `말씀 묵상`: sermon summaries, sermon comparisons, and personal meditation
  rooted in a passage.
- `신앙 질문`: exploratory theological or faith questions whose uncertainty
  or reasoning is central.
- `성경 읽기`: Bible text, chapter reading notes, and reference-oriented
  scripture material rather than a sermon meditation.
